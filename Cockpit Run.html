CANYON RUN: UFO HUNT

Press C to switch between chase view and cockpit view. Hold Shift to speed up and Space to auto-fire at the alien UFOs (they take several hits, bosses take many more and shoot back, and crashing into one is instant death). Upgrade your guns in the shop (let go and the engine fades). At ~120 km/h hold W to climb; let go and the nose tilts down and you descend. S dives. A/D bank and turn left/right, and the plane levels out when you release. Touch: drag up/down/sideways to steer, +/− throttle. Gamepad works too. Touching the ground after takeoff, flying too far off to the side, or hitting anything ends the run. Tilt up too steeply or slow down too much and you stall. The farther you go, the faster and harder it gets. Too slow = stall, too low = crash. Fly through the orange rings for points (dead center is worth double) and spend points on upgrades. Dodge towers and rocks.
